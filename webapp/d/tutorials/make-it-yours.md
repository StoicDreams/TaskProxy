## What you'll do

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

1. Pick a theme.
2. Set text size, motion, and other accessibility choices.
3. Give each project an icon, and switch projects from their tiles.

Your theme and accessibility choices are kept in Task Proxy on this computer. A project's icon is saved in the project, so your team sees it too.

</webui-page-segment>

## 1. Pick a theme

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Open **Tools**, the toolbox at the top right, and choose its **Theme** tab, the palette.

There are 48 themes in eight groups: **Classic**, **Colors**, **Organic**, **Electric**, **Earth**, **Sky**, **Space**, and **Ocean**, each with three light and three dark. A theme is more than its colors: each has its own heading font, a texture behind the app, a mark beside headings, and its own look for the sections of a page. Each tile shows them, so you can see a theme before you choose it.

Click a tile, and the whole app changes at once.

</webui-page-segment>

## 2. Set accessibility choices

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Choose the **Accessibility** tab of **Tools**:

| Choice | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| **Text size** | Makes text larger or smaller across the app, from 80% to 160%, the terminal's included |
| **Reduce motion** | Panels, menus, and tabs change without sliding or fading. On to begin with when your system asks for less motion |
| **Underline links** | Links stand out from the text around them by more than their color |
| **Stronger focus outline** | What the keyboard is on gets a thicker outline that is easier to follow |
| **Show tooltips** | Buttons say what they do when the pointer rests on them. On to begin with |

They apply straight away, and from the moment Task Proxy starts. **Reset** goes back to the usual settings.

</webui-page-segment>

## 3. Give your projects icons

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

A project's icon is one of its own images, such as its logo.

1. Open **Settings** from the Task Proxy logo at the top left.
2. Click the icon beside the current project's name, or beside a project in the **Projects** list.
3. Pick one of the project's images from the grid. Each is named, so you can tell similar ones apart.

The icon is saved in the project's `.taskproxy/Project.json`, so everyone who opens the project sees it. A project with no images can have one added, such as an SVG or PNG.

</webui-page-segment>

## 4. Switch projects from their tiles

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

The **Project** dropdown at the top of the left navigation opens a grid of tiles, one for each project, with its icon and git status, so you can see which projects have changes before you open one.
